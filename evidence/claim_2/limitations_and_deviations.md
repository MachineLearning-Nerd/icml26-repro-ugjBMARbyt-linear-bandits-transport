# Claim 2 limitations and deviations

The verdict concerns the literal v1 displayed regret definition. With the
standard per-round comparator subtraction, this particular contradiction
disappears; the experiment explicitly records that corrected control rather
than implying the standard OFUL theorem is false.

The horizon is evaluated analytically, not by looping 100 million times. All
quantities are closed-form or finite-dimensional determinant bounds, so such a
loop would add cost without evidence.
