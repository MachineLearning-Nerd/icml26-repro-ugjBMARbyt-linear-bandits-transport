# Claim 5 limitations and deviations

The finite 3×3 schedule stops being misspecified once its third coefficient is
included, so its finite-window slope is not presented as an asymptotic
counterexample. The verdict instead rests on exact counterexamples to the
premise-to-tail implication used by the universal corollary.

No conclusion is drawn about a repaired coefficient assumption, a conventional
Sobolev/Besov source condition, or the changed v2 exponent.
