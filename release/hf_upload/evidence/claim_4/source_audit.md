# Claim 4 source audit

V1 Assumption 3 lower-bounds a cumulative coefficient `l1` sum divided by
`zeta(n)`. Corollary 5.3 substitutes the discontinuous indicator
`zeta(n)=1_{n>=N}` and the appendix labels this as equivalent to all
coefficients after `N` being zero. That equivalence does not follow: for
`n>=N`, a sufficiently large cumulative head satisfies the lower bound
regardless of the tail.

The indicator also conflicts with Assumption 3's stated continuity requirement.
The experiment evaluates the intended integer-order interpretation rather than
taking the corollary as vacuously true.
